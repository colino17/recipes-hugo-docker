1. Cut beef into large chunks, season with salt and brown in a frying pan. Once browned remove and set aside.
2. Remove the stems and seeds from the dried chilies and sear lightly in olive oil for several minutes.
4. Add olive oil and onion to the frying pan and cook until onions start to brown.
5. Add garlic and cook until garlic has softened.
6. Add tomatoes and brown sugar to the frying pan and cook until the tomatoes begin to break down. Add beef broth as needed.
7. Add the peppers to the frying pan. Add beef broth as needed.
8. Mix in the apple cider vinegar, lime juice, oregano, paprika, cumin and cloves.
9. Once the tomatoes and peppers have broken down and everything is well combined, remove from heat and let cool.
10. Once cooled, transfer the sauce to a blender and blend until smooth.
11. Add the beef, sauce, bay leaves, cinnamon stick and more beef broth to a dutch oven and cook at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 3 hours. Check regularly and add beef broth as needed.
12. Remove the beef from the sauce and shred.
13. Strain sauce to remove thickness and form a consome.
14. Refrigerate the consome until the fat separates.
15. Add the fat to a skillet and fry tortillas until they start to crisp on one side (alternatively spray the tortillas with cooking oil).
16. Flip over the tortilla and add the cheese.
17. Continue frying ufor several seconds and then add the meat to one side of the tortilla.
18. Flip in half and allow the cheese to melt and become gooey before flipping it carefully onto the opposite side. Brush lightly with consome. Remove from heat once golden on both sides.
20. Add some fresh chopped cilantro and a splash of lime juice to the top of the tacos.
21. Serve with a bowl of the consomme sauce for dipping.
