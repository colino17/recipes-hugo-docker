    amount: 1

    amount: 2
    unit: tbsp
  - name: Sesame Oil
    amount: 1
    unit: tbsp
  - name: Chicken Broth
    amount: 1
    unit: tbsp
side_image: ./images/chinese-broccoli.jpg
---
1. Cut up broccoli into florets and steam for 4-5 minutes or until it has just a slight crunch/firmness. Remove from steamer.
2. Add butter and sesame oil to a saute pan and melt.
3. Add garlic and simmer over medium heat for about one minute until garlic starts to soften.
4. Add soy sauce and chicken broth. Continue simmering until reduced by half.
5. Add steamed broccoli and toss until coated.
