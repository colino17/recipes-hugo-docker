1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces and marinade with sesame oil, 2 tbsp soy sauce, and 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar.
2. Add flour, cornstarch, salt, and pepper to a large bowl.
3. Strain the marinated chicken, retaining the marinade in another large bowl.
4. Add the marinated chicken to the dredge bowl and toss until fully coated.
5. Add the dredged chicken back to the leftover marinade and toss.
6. Toss the chicken in the dredge mixture again until fully coated again.
7. Place chicken in a single layer in the air fryer basket and spray with cooking oil.
8. Cook in the air fryer at 370F for 14 minutes. Remove and set aside.
9. Add the butter, red pepper flakes and garlic to a large saute pan. Cook for approximately one minute until the garlic starts to soften.
10. Add honey, and the remaining soy sauce and apple cider vinegar to the pan and simmer until reduced slightly.
11. Add chicken and toss until fully coated.
12. Serve with rice or chinese broccoli.
