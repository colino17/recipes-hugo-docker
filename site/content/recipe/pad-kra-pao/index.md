1. Crush garlic and Thai chilies into a paste using a mortar and pestle. 
2. Saute garlic and thai chilies in oil for a few minutes.
3. Break up ground beef and cook on one side until well browned and mild crusting.
4. Stir beef and cook through.
5. Reduce heat and add hoisin sauce, soy sauce, and black pepper and toss until well coated.
6. Add sliced sweet peppers and mix thoroughly.
7. Add basil and mix thoroughly. Cook until the basil is slightly wilted.
